# Calculations for Question 4

## 4(a) Thermal Voltage at 25°C

The thermal voltage is approximately:

V_T = 25.9 mV = 0.0259 V

## 4(b) Diode Current

Given:

- I_S = 40 nA
- n = 2
- V_D = 0.5 V
- V_T = 0.0259 V

Diode equation:

I_D = I_S (e^(V_D / (nV_T)) - 1)

Substitute the values:

I_D = 40 × 10^-9 (e^(0.5 / (2 × 0.0259)) - 1)

First calculate the denominator:

2 × 0.0259 = 0.0518

Then:

0.5 / 0.0518 ≈ 9.65

So:

I_D = 40 × 10^-9 (e^9.65 - 1)

e^9.65 ≈ 15500

Therefore:

I_D ≈ 40 × 10^-9 × 15499
I_D ≈ 6.2 × 10^-4 A

Final Answer:

I_D ≈ 0.62 mA

# Written Answers

## Question 1
N-type semiconductor is made by doping a pure semiconductor with atoms that have five valence electrons, such as phosphorus. This adds extra electrons, so electrons become the majority carriers.

P-type semiconductor is made by doping with atoms that have three valence electrons, such as boron. This creates holes in the material, so holes become the majority carriers.

## Question 2
In forward bias, the p-side of the diode is connected to the positive terminal and the n-side is connected to the negative terminal. This reduces the depletion region and allows current to flow more easily.

In reverse bias, the p-side is connected to the negative terminal and the n-side is connected to the positive terminal. This increases the depletion region and blocks most current, except for a very small leakage current.

## Question 3
An ideal device is a theoretical device that behaves perfectly according to its basic model. It has no losses, no imperfections, and no unwanted effects. For example, an ideal diode would conduct perfectly in forward bias and block all current in reverse bias.

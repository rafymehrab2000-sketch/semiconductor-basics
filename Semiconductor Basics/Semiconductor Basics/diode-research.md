# Diode Research

## Diode Name
1N4148

## Manufacturer
ON Semiconductor

## Forward Voltage
The forward voltage of the 1N4148 diode is typically around 0.7 V during normal operation. According to the datasheet, when the forward current is about 10 mA, the voltage across the diode can reach approximately 1 V. This voltage drop is common for silicon diodes and represents the voltage required for the diode to start conducting current.

## Maximum Reverse Voltage
The maximum reverse voltage for the 1N4148 diode is 100 V. This means the diode can safely block reverse voltage up to this level without breaking down or allowing significant current to flow.

## Maximum Forward Current
The diode can handle a maximum forward current of about 200 mA. If the current exceeds this limit, the diode may overheat and become damaged.

## Operating Temperature Range
The operating temperature range of the 1N4148 diode is from −65°C to 175°C. This wide temperature range allows the diode to operate reliably in different environmental conditions.

## Real Application
The 1N4148 diode is widely used in fast switching circuits and signal processing applications. Because it can switch on and off very quickly, it is useful in digital electronics, protection circuits, and communication systems.
